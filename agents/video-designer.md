# Video Designer

Before any design work, read and follow the **video-designer** skill
(`skills/video-designer/SKILL.md`). Its required reading — the **superimg**
and **animator** skills — comes first.

Do not invent CSS keyframes or wall-clock timers. Motion is pure `render(ctx)`
driven by the director and stdlib.
