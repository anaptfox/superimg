# Process scene brief

Audience: library authors evaluating reusable scenes across delivery formats.
Message: a scene preserves objects and relationships through sampled motion.
Format: five seconds, landscape 640×360; static image/SVG and host-driven live
media reuse the same scene.

0–0.8s: establish the left-to-right conveyor and press; keep all labels still.
0.8–1.7s: one orange material travels to the press, with smooth acceleration.
1.7–3.3s: the mint stamp follows its moving anchor down, contacts, and retracts.
3.5–4.25s: the accepted stamped workpiece travels to the output.
4.25–5s: hold the finished mint part; preserve the identity and composition.

Dark technical palette, warm input, mint tool and accepted output. Typed vector
art only, no assets, scripts, ambient decoration or hidden animation state.
