# Aster Basin

A fictional tidal city forms from copper grains on slate water. Three eras
teach the viewer its causeway, cistern court, and split-octagon tidal archive.
A continuous GPS journey then visits that world before the camera rises to
compare two imagined alternatives: Raised Shore and Open Shore.

The alternatives share a present baseline and a locked camera. Raised Shore
returns to Present before Open Shore forms. A faint slate perimeter preserves
Raised Shore during the comparison hold. Speculation dissolves first; the
present city then erodes, leaving cyan route and landmark traces.

The hero and annotated teaching cut are 50 seconds, 3840×2160, 30 fps. Both
retain essential navigation and future labels. Teaching only adds short
captions. The interactive example starts paused at Present overview and uses
the same world, grain ids, route, and camera sampler.

Close street images should retain individual grain facets and irregular
courses. Matte facade opacity is capped below 25%. A procedural geometry
probe cannot establish material readability: inspect actual 4K detail crops
and the decoded master before accepting the film.

Sound uses resonant filtered material noise and a modal buoy tone. Original
score assets are baked offline; interactive playback begins silent.
