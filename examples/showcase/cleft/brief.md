# Cleft

An original identity study: two interlocking slabs retain a stepped diagonal
negative-space cut while ink, granular mineral, and etched graphite replace
their surface paint. No existing product identity or Foundation asset is used.

The viewer should recognize the frontal silhouette before the camera pushes
into the cut. Relief and individual grains should read in the 4K macro. The
return must land on the exact frontal placement; the paint tour then shows
what the shared geometry can carry.

The hero is 18 seconds at 3840×2160, 30 fps. The teaching cut adds short captions
without changing visual choreography or score. Additional deliveries are a
six-second sting, four-second 120-frame loop, 2160-square loop edition and
poster, and a 64-pixel flat SVG icon.

The live eight-second pointer timeline is a separate silent definition.
Slider and preset controls suspend pointer drive while changing the pose.
Reduced motion shows a held pose rather than leaving the mark half revealed.

Sound combines an original stone modal impact with bowed glass. Loop audio
must wrap its tails. Score timing and final mux require separate acceptance;
silent review clips cannot validate sound.

Geometric probes describe author-reported coordinates and counts. They do not
establish rendered contrast, grain readability, icon recognition, or loop craft.
